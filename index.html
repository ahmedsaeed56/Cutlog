      Your BMI over time

      From the weights you log in Today, using your height from Targets.

      How to measure accurately

      • Weight: morning, after the toilet, before eating or drinking, same scale each time.
      • Height: no shoes, heels against a wall, looking straight ahead.
      • Waist: tape level around the belly, midway between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone (near the belly button), snug but not pressing, at the end of a normal breath out. Measure twice and use the average.
      • Hip: around the widest part of the buttocks, feet together.
      • Neck: just below the Adam's apple, tape sloping slightly down at the front.

      This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Under 18s need age-and-sex BMI percentiles instead, and pregnancy changes everything here — speak to a doctor.
